## 1. Skill vs. Chance Doctrine in Indian Jurisprudence

Under landmark judgments of the Supreme Court of India:
- **State of Bombay v. R.M.D. Chamarbaugwala (AIR 1957 SC 699)**: Competitions where success depends significantly upon the exercise of skill are not "gambling", and are constitutionally protected commercial speech and trade under **Article 19(1)(g)** of the Constitution of India.
- **K.R. Lakshmanan v. State of Tamil Nadu (1996 2 SCC 226)**: A game of skill is defined as one where, although the element of chance cannot be entirely eliminated, success depends primarily upon the superior knowledge, training, attention, experience and adroitness of the player.

### Application to GameEarn Pro Games:
- **Trivia Quiz**: Pure test of knowledge and reaction time (Skill).
- **Number Tile Puzzle & Memory Match**: Spatial memory and algorithmic logic (Skill).
- **Math Sprint**: Arithmetic computational speed under time pressure (Skill).
- **Bubble Shooter**: Vector trajectory prediction and reflex coordination (Skill).
- **Spin Wheel & Scratch Card**: Structured as promotional loyalty rewards (free-to-play) rather than paid wagering lotteries.

## 3. Taxation & Withholding Obligations (Section 194BA)

Under the Finance Act 2023:
1. **Section 194BA of the Income Tax Act**:
   - Mandates deduction of **30% TDS** on the **Net Winnings** of the user at the time of withdrawal or at the end of the financial year.
2. **Formula for Net Winnings**:
   $$\text{Net Winnings} = (\text{Total Withdrawals} + \text{Closing Wallet Balance}) - (\text{Total Deposits} + \text{Opening Wallet Balance})$$
   *(Since GameEarn Pro is free-to-play with 0 deposits, all withdrawals constitute promotional earnings)*.
3. **KYC Requirement**:
   - Permanent Account Number (PAN) collection and verification is required prior to processing disbursements exceeding statutory reporting thresholds.
